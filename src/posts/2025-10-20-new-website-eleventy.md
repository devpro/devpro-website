I'm thrilled to announce that the completely new version of devpro.fr is officially online!
It has been built with Eleventy and Pico CSS.

<!-- more -->

After five days of exploring and building with new tools, I've created this static website using [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/), a fantastic static site generator, and [Pico CSS](https://picocss.com/), a lightweight and elegant CSS framework.

It was a fun and rewarding journey to get this site up and running.

I would love to hear your feedback on the site, take a look around and [contact me](/contact) to share what you think!

You can also check the [source code](https://github.com/devpro/devpro-website) on GitHub.

N.B. Over time, I'll be decommissioning my old blog website, [blog-bertrand-thomas.devpro.fr](https://blog-bertrand-thomas.devpro.fr/), which contains my previous posts.
